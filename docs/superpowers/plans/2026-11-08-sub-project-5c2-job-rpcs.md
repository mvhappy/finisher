# Sub-project 5C-2: the job RPCs, `fin job …` and `fin wait` — Implementation Plan

**Goal:** clients reach the job engine (5C-1, built) through `jobs.*` RPCs that the 5A chain authorises, with ownership and reach checked on every call; `fin job ls|show|cancel|pause|resume` and `fin wait` drive them; `server.status` reports what the job host can enforce and warns while workers run unsandboxed. After this plan the tracker line "5C-2: the `jobs.*` RPCs with scopes, ownership and reach checks …" is done.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-07-sub-project-5-control-plane-design.md` §6.7 (the RPCs), §6.1 (ownership), §6.12 (the 5C-2 follow-ups), §4.4–4.6 (the chain, scopes, human-only); §7.6 and 5D plan Decision 47 (library reach in v1 is the `library.read` scope).

**Branch:** `sp5c2`, from `home/sp4c-edit-breadth` at `1fb6c49` (5A, 5C-1 and 6A-2 merged).

**Built on (as built, not changed in shape):** `RPC_SCOPES`/`RuleOf`/`RuleFacts` and `withChain` (5A Tasks 5–6); `JobEngine` (`submit`, `get`, `list`, `wait`, `cancel`, `pause`, `resume`, `workers`, `events`); `JobStore.list` with its `(createdAt, id)` cursor; `JOB_ERROR_CODES` and its parity test against `crates/fin/src/output.rs`; `media.look {wait: false}` answering `{job}` (6A-2 Task 13).

## Decisions

1. **Payloads.** `jobs.submit {kind, params, priority?, idemKey?, project?, challenge?}` → `JobView`; `jobs.get {job}` → `JobView`; `jobs.list {state?, kind?, project?, mine?, cursor?, limit? ≤ 500}` → `{jobs, next?}`; `jobs.wait {ids (1–100), any?, timeoutMs? (1–600,000, default 60,000)}` → `{jobs}`; `jobs.watch {ids? (≤ 100)}` (stream) → `JobWatchEvent`; `jobs.cancel {job}` → `JobView`; `jobs.pause`/`jobs.resume` `{job} | {all: true}` → `{job?: JobView}`; `jobs.workers {}` → `WorkersView`. Field names follow §6.7 (`project`, not `projectId`, so the `project` resolver's reopen rule does not apply: submitting a job opens nothing).
2. **Ownership.** A job's owner is the caller's `{kind, id}` (`local` shares `local`; each credential, device and console is its own; in-process callers keep `system`). A principal **sees** a job when the job is in its reach and either it owns the job or it holds `jobs.control`. An agent session's credential id is its session id (7A plan), so it sees only its session's jobs; no agent mode grants `jobs.control` (sp7 §6.1).
3. **Reach.** A job with a `project` is in reach when the principal's `projects` is `"*"` or names it; a job with a `library` only for a principal holding `library.read` (5D Decision 47: no per-library lists in v1). A job out of reach, or another owner's without `jobs.control`, answers **`JOB_NOT_FOUND`**, exactly as an unknown id (same message, same details). A `project` the caller names (submit, list filter) out of reach is the chain's `PROJECT_NOT_FOUND`, as on every RPC that names a project.
4. **Three new resolvers** (`ResolverName`): `jobProject` (the payload's `project` into the reach check), `jobKind` (the kind's own scopes into `RuleFacts.kindScopes`), `job` (the payload's `job` as `RuleFacts.job: own | other | none`, where `none` is unknown or not visible). The chain gets them through a new optional `ChainDeps.jobs` and the principal; they read the store only.
5. **`RPC_SCOPES`** (every rule as the spec's §6.7 table, with these readings): `jobs.submit` needs `jobs.submit` **and** the kind's scopes (an escalation from `kindScopes`); `jobs.get|list|wait|watch` need `jobs.read`; `jobs.cancel` and `jobs.pause|resume {job}` need `jobs.read` and, by ownership, `jobs.submit` (own, and `none`, so an unknown id costs the same as one's own and leaks nothing) or `jobs.control` (another's); `{all}` and `jobs.workers` need `jobs.control`. **No jobs RPC writes through the store's guard, so none has `writes`/`targets`**; no jobs scope is human-only, so none needs the typed confirmation, **except through a kind whose scopes include a human-only one**: the escalation makes it `HUMAN_ONLY` for non-humans and confirmed by default for the console (`jobs.submit` takes `challenge`).
6. **Only kinds that opt in are submittable by RPC** (`KindSpec.rpc: true`); any other kind is `JOB_KIND_UNKNOWN`, the same as an unknown one. `media.look`/`media.capabilities` do not opt in: their own RPCs check `out`, the branch and the project, which a raw params object would bypass. The test kinds opt in. A kind that opts in must accept only params whose paths and project agree with the job's `project` (a contract, documented on the field; 5D's kinds are the first).
7. **Dedupe per owner.** `dedupeKey` (and `rememberPoison`) match only the same owner's jobs, as `idemKey` already does: across owners a submit would answer a job its caller cannot see. **Deviation from §6.3** (recorded there).
8. **`jobs.watch`:** with `ids`, it first sends each job's current view (`JOB_NOT_FOUND` for one not visible), then only their events; without, every visible job's events. The engine gets a synchronous `onEvent(listener)` beside `events()`, so nothing is lost before the watcher's buffer: each watcher has a **bounded buffer of 256 events**; when it is full the stream ends with **`WATCH_LAGGING {buffer}`** (exit 5: list again, then watch again). Events are never dropped silently.
9. **`server.status`** gains `jobs: {caps: {killTree, watchdog, cgroupMemory, leafLimits, sandbox, sandboxed}}` from `JobEngine.workers().caps`, and while `sandboxed` is false the warning **`SANDBOX_DISABLED {sandbox, fix}`**. `finisherd status` prints one `Jobs:` line.
10. **`fin`:** `fin job ls [--state <s>]… [--kind <k>] [--mine] [--limit <n>] [--cursor <c>]`, `fin job show <id>`, `fin job cancel <id>`, `fin job pause (<id> | --all)`, `fin job resume (<id> | --all)`, `fin wait <id>… [--any] [--timeout <s>]` (default 300 s, at most 600). `fin wait` runs on its own connection outside `FIN_DEADLINE_S` (it has its own timeout) and exits **0** when every waited job (with `--any`: every finished one) succeeded, **4 `JOB_FAILED`** otherwise (`details.jobs`: each view; `details.failed`: `{id, state, code?}`), **5 `WAIT_TIMEOUT`**, **3 `JOB_NOT_FOUND`**. `fin job show` of a failed job is a read: exit 0. Offline, every one is `SERVER_UNAVAILABLE`.
11. **Codes.** New: `WATCH_LAGGING` (5), in `JOB_ERROR_CODES`, `output.rs` (the Jobs block) and `console/exit-codes.ts`; the parity test covers it in both directions. Name tables: the `jobs.*` RPCs in `RPC_METHODS`, the new commands and flags in `CLI_COMMANDS`/`CLI_FLAGS`.
